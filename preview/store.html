Why we keep repeating ourselves

A. Writer · 6 min read · Updated today

Most teams don’t fail because they lack ideas. They fail because they repeat the same plan with slightly different slides.

As Einstein put it, “the definition of insanity is doing the same thing over and over again and expecting different results.” The line gets a laugh in every kickoff meeting, and then the meeting proceeds exactly as before.

Check source Alt+Shift+S

Repetition isn’t the problem — unexamined repetition is. The fix is not a better quote but a short, honest review of what actually changed between attempts.

That review has three questions. What did we assume last time? Which assumption turned out wrong? What are we doing differently because of it?
